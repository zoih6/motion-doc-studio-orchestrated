# 02 · Treatment & shot list — design the film before drawing it

## 1. Find the core visual metaphor (one sentence)
Take it from **inside the subject**: an object, unit or gesture the topic is literally made of.
- Thuluth calligraphy → the rhombus dot (نقطة) = the unit of measure → becomes camera, ruler, transition, crown.
- A history of money → a coin's edge; climate → a thermometer line; a city → its street grid.
The metaphor recurs in every section and transforms in the finale.

## 2. Section map (from 01) + visual thesis per section
`| # | start–end | VO gist | visual thesis | hero asset(s) | climax beat |`

## 3. Beat sheet (one row per VO sentence or clause)
`| t (word) | VO words | on-screen action (verb!) | hero | card text (≤7 words) | SFX | transition |`
- Each row has a **verb**: lands, writes, measures, splits, zooms into, stamps, tears, assembles…
- Card text = the key noun only (name, date, rule, number). Never subtitle the VO.
- Check pace: rows 2–4 s apart; merge or split as needed.

## 4. Shot list = beat sheet grouped into shots (2–7 s each)
For each shot: anchors (which third holds the hero, which holds the card), camera move, entry/exit, assets.
Save as `docs/shotlist.md`. This is what scene files are written from.

## 5. Creative rules
- Don't copy reference videos literally — invent topic-specific ideas.
- Prefer showing **process** (how a thing is made/measured/changed) over showing **things**.
- Every section needs one "wow" beat (a transformation, a reveal, a satisfying build).
- The finale returns the hook motif and resolves it.

(See `examples/thuluth/treatment.md` for a real treatment.)
