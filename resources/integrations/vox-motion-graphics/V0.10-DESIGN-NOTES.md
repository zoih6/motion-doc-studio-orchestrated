# v0.10 — Visual Invention & Long-Form Direction

## Audit and pre-edit architecture

Source: standalone repository at revision `5632e4f`, identical to the installed skill before upgrade. Full audit covered SKILL, all references, schemas, tools, tests, examples, version and release history. Existing relationship decisions, shot scores, purposeful holds, provenance and publication receipts already work as contracts; extend rather than replace them. Production observations from Kodak and Concorde motivate general directing gaps, not subject-specific bans or a proven model-quality gain.

### Ownership and order

`brief/evidence → film/chapter/sequence direction + vocabulary → mechanism concepts/shot scores → storyboard → independent scene/sequence/chapter/film critique → freeze → final VO/alignment → conformance → bounded implementation handoff → mastered draft → technical + multi-level editorial review → publication`

- **Film:** viewer promise, argument, emotional progression, callbacks and demonstrated conclusion.
- **Chapter (when useful):** major argument turn, evidence burden and chapter payoff.
- **Sequence:** neighboring-scene rhythm, recurring motifs, vocabulary, escalation and local payoff. Shorts collapse chapter/sequence ownership into the film; they need no artificial chapters.
- **Scene:** one visual contribution, construction family, staging, conditional state delta and attention handoff.
- **Beat:** semantic event or useful hold within the scene; final VO remains its timing authority.

One new directing reference owns hierarchy, sequence pass and macro-payoff. Existing references own mechanisms, assets, motion, transitions, storyboard and critique. Keep the main skill a routing entrypoint, not a second rule catalog.

### Contracts

- `storyboard.direction`: film plus ordered sequences and optional chapters. Sequence scene IDs partition film order; chapters, when present, partition sequence order. A collapsed short uses one compact sequence in machine handoffs, not extra approval pages.
- Plan vocabulary before acquisition as subject identity + visual role/viewpoint + representation + later asset bindings. Existing asset ledger retains acquisition, acceptance, rights, derivation and provenance ownership. Unresolved vocabulary may have empty asset bindings during planning; freeze resolves used roles.
- `visualJob` makes explanatory scenes require `stateDelta {before, operation, after, viewerInference}`. Identification, evidence, chronology, atmosphere and emotion are exempt; a hold inside an explanatory scene remains valid. The critic judges the perceptible relationship change, not the presence of four strings.
- Difficult or important explanatory beats compare 2–3 low-cost concepts from different construction families before expensive sourcing/coding. Store only candidates, selected ID and selection reason when structured handoff is useful.
- Scene rhythm profiles support a sequence scan of construction, scale, camera, object, visual/information density, value/color, motion energy and media mode. Sequence repetition assessment explains intentional repetitions; no automated diversity quota or same-family ban.
- Structured handoff and payoff choices record observable intent, including hard cuts, contrast, sound-led transitions, typography and stillness.
- Independent editorial reports retain scene findings and add sequence/chapter/film scope findings plus actual viewing-size evidence. Rename the universal `stateChangeMeaningful` check to job-sensitive `visualJobSatisfied` so stillness never requires a fictional delta.
- Future `ScenePacket` / `SequencePacket` share a bounded packet schema and reference frozen revision, local evidence/assets, relevant style/timing and adjacent handoffs. No compiler, router, model SDK or new runtime dependency in this release.
- Legacy projects remain draft-valid. v0.10 publication requires direction and full scoped review; migrate metadata and independently review actual artifacts before refreshing digests. Never manufacture approval to migrate.

## Implemented decisions and final review

The shared vocabulary lives in `direction.vocabulary`; scenes select relevant roles with `vocabularyIds`. Sequence intent requires `purpose`, `payoffTrajectory`, `rhythmIntent` and `repetitionAssessment`; a one-line quiet/uniform plan is valid. Film callbacks and chapters remain optional. Scene `rhythmProfile` supplies scan dimensions without numeric thresholds or diversity quotas.

Scoped findings stay in the existing artifact-bound design/editorial reports. Per-scene player-size evidence carries positive dimensions, distance, observation, local `proofRef` and `proofSha256`. The validator binds proof bytes; independent review verifies their relation to the current board/master and actual readability. Vocabulary-only used assets undergo the same readiness, rights and provenance gates as directly referenced assets. Natural-language role equality is not a gate.

Packets remain bounded preparation contracts, not a runtime compiler: `ScenePacket` has one scene, `SequencePacket` one or more ordered scenes. Schema validation checks structure; consuming implementers verify frozen approval, IDs/order, final alignment, byte identity and beat windows. The original board and asset ledger remain authoritative.

### Contradictions resolved

- Fixed vertical/seven-item storyboard assumptions → target-ratio, grouped hierarchical overview.
- Universal motion QA → job-sensitive `visualJobSatisfied`, conditional delta and valid stillness.
- Optional prose versus required machine rhythm intent → one consistent, lightweight required intent.
- Exact asset-role text equality → editorial shot fitness with ID/provenance enforcement.
- Only unresolved label beats needing concept search → difficult or important explanatory beats, without redoing a completed search.
- Packet documentation claiming byte/window checks → explicit schema-only checks and consumer obligations.
- Validator help ordering reading after all action → actual readable windows may overlap motion/transition.

### Independent architecture review

| Envelope | Ownership and pacing | Continuity and payoff | Implementation and review | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 45–60s vertical | Collapse upper hierarchy; compact sequence, scene/beat direction and purposeful holds | Phone-scale vocabulary/motif continuity and a legible demonstrated or justified payoff | Bounded scene packets; independent scene/sequence/film review and release evidence | Pass |
| ~5min landscape | Sequences own argument turns, density/energy contrast and evidence reading; chapters only when useful | Stable identities, varied useful staging, attention handoffs and cumulative local payoffs | Desktop/mobile player evidence; aligned local implementation; scoped independent master review | Pass |
| 10–20min documentary/explainer | Chapter/sequence owners manage evidence burden, quiet/busy sections and escalation without fixed counts | Recurring motifs, callbacks, semantic contrast and whole-film comprehension/payoff | Scope-limited packets; chapter/sequence/film critique plus unchanged provenance/timing/publication gates | Pass |

These are architecture verdicts, not rendered-film quality scores. Independent code review found no remaining introduced defects in reviewed paths after repairs.
